Saturn Tracktor 1.0.0
Gevork Saturn

Saturn Tracktor includes its own UI and managed DSP implementation.
It uses NAudio 2.2.1 (MIT) and Microsoft WebView2 SDK 1.0.4258.31
(Microsoft redistribution license). See bundled license texts.

The unchanged FxSound virtual audio driver 14.1.0.0 and fxdevcon helper
are distributed as independent third-party components. The friendly endpoint
name is changed through Windows audio properties to Saturn Audio Enhancer;
the signed driver package, vendor identity and certificates are not modified.

Driver package and helper were extracted from the signed official installer:
https://download.fxsound.com/fxsoundlatest
FxSound application source (including fxdevcon installer helper):
https://github.com/fxsound2/fxsound-app/tree/d8e7a23d37ed5939c2a3090a1c1756c7f2500b17
FxSound virtual driver source:
https://github.com/fxsound2/fxsound-driver
FxSound licenses: AGPL-3.0 and the included Microsoft driver sample license.
Corresponding driver source and installer helper source are included in
SaturnTracktor-source.zip supplied alongside this executable.
